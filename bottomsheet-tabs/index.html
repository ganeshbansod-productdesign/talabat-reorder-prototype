Interaction

  • Scroll down the list past 24px and the tabs collapse: images fade out, labels stay as a compact sticky bar.
  • Scroll back to the top and the tabs expand again.
  • Tap a tab to switch category. The list resets to the top, so the tabs expand.
  • The tab bar stays pinned; only the product list scrolls.

Motion

  • 280ms ease for height, spacing and underline width.
  • Hysteresis: collapse at > 24px, expand at < 4px, so it doesn’t flicker at the threshold.
  • Respects reduced motion.

Content

Products, prices and images come from the Figma frames. Only “Top items” has designed content, so the other tabs reuse the same products in a different order.
